titra — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 (rev. 7, for titra 2.0.2 and later: corrections on analytics, identifiers, Health import and deletion; note on earlier versions)

The short version. titra works without an account. Your log is stored on your phone, and titra uploads it only if you turn on titra+ cloud backup. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use advertising identifiers. Usage analytics are off unless you switch them on, and they never contain your doses, weights or symptoms. Crash reports are always sent, and the app checks your subscription status each time it opens; neither includes your health entries. This policy describes titra 2.0.2 and later; see Earlier versions for what was different before.

Who we are

titra is made by Agent M sp. z o.o., a company registered in Poland under KRS number 0001259388, which is the data controller for the information described here. Contact: info@titrapp.com.

What is stored on your phone

Your medication, doses, injection sites, pain scores, weights, side effects, daily food-noise ratings and settings are stored in a database on your device. Unless you use cloud backup, we cannot see them. Deleting the app removes them from your phone. You → Delete all data deletes the log and settings in the app, and the titra 1.x copy of your log if you updated from 1.x. If you are signed in, it also signs you out of cloud backup; the copy in your backup stays until you delete your account. Backups made by your phone itself (for example iCloud Backup) are controlled by your device settings, not by titra, and may include the app's data.

Health Connect and Apple Health

If you have titra+ and switch on Import weight from Health Connect (Android) or Import weight from Apple Health (iOS), titra asks for permission to read your weight, and nothing else. It never writes to Health Connect or Apple Health and never reads any other type of data. Weigh-ins are copied into titra's database on your phone each time you open the app, going back to one week before your first logged dose, or up to one year if you have not logged a dose. They are treated like weigh-ins you typed: they are uploaded only if you also use cloud backup, and are then stored by Google Firebase with the rest of your log. Data from Health Connect and Apple Health is never used for advertising and never sold, and it is passed to no one other than Google Firebase as part of your cloud backup, if you use it. Switch the import off in titra, or remove titra's access in Health Connect or the iOS Health app, at any time; weigh-ins already copied stay in titra until you delete them.

We do not use advertising networks, attribution SDKs or the advertising identifier. On Android the advertising-ID permission is removed from the app.

Legal bases (UK GDPR / EU GDPR)

  • Health data in cloud backup: your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)), given when you turn on cloud backup. You can withdraw it at any time: signing out stops further uploads, and deleting your account deletes the backup.
  • Usage analytics: your consent, given with the in-app switch, which is off by default.
  • Crash reporting: our legitimate interest in a working app. Crash reports contain no health entries.
  • Subscriptions and your account: performance of our contract with you.

International transfers

Cloud backup data is stored in Google's Europe multi-region. Google, Crashlytics, Analytics and RevenueCat may process data in the United States. Where data leaves the UK or EEA we rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and otherwise on Standard Contractual Clauses with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

How long we keep it

  • Cloud backup and account: until you delete your account. Entries you delete one at a time are hidden and kept, marked as deleted, on your phone and in your backup; Delete all data and deleting your account remove them for good.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Analytics events: up to 14 months (Google Analytics retention setting).
  • Purchase records: as long as needed for billing, refunds and legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

  • Use titra without an account; your log is not uploaded.
  • Export your log as CSV, or delete it from your phone, from You in the app.
  • Delete your cloud account in the app (You → Cloud backup → Delete account) or by email — see how to delete your account.
  • You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email info@titrapp.com. You can complain to the Polish data protection authority (UODO), the UK ICO, or your local authority.
  • US residents, including under Washington's My Health My Data Act: we do not sell or share consumer health data, and you can request access or deletion at the same address.

Security

Data sent to Google and RevenueCat is encrypted in transit, and Google encrypts stored data at rest. Cloud backup is not end-to-end encrypted: Google holds the keys. On your phone the data relies on your device's own security.

Children

titra is not intended for anyone under 18.

Earlier versions

  • titra 2.0 and 2.0.1 (iOS builds up to 47, Android builds up to 94). Google Analytics for Firebase started with Google's default settings the first time the app was opened, and the app switched it off a moment later, once it had read your settings. In that interval Google's library may have recorded and sent its automatic start-up events (such as first open) with an app-instance identifier. On iPhone and iPad these builds also contained advertising-measurement components of Google's Analytics library, including its advertising-identifier component; the app has no tracking prompt, so that component could not obtain the identifier. Version 2.0.2 keeps Analytics off until you switch it on and is built without that component.
  • titra 1.x. Versions before 2.0 used different service providers for cloud sync, analytics, crash reporting and the measurement of installs from adverts, for which the iPhone version asked for permission to track. None of them is part of titra 2.0 or later. To ask what was held about you under 1.x, or to have it deleted, email info@titrapp.com.
