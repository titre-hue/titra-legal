titra — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026 (rev. 6, for titra 2.0: food noise log, Health weight import)

The short version. titra works without an account. Everything you log stays on your phone unless you turn on titra+ cloud backup. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use advertising identifiers. Usage analytics are off unless you switch them on, and they never contain your doses, weights or symptoms.

Who we are

titra is made by AgentM sp. z o.o., a company registered in Poland, which is the data controller for the information described here. Contact: info@titrapp.com.

What stays on your phone

Your medication, doses, injection sites, pain scores, weights, side effects, daily food-noise ratings and settings are stored in a database on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app, or using You → Delete all data, removes them.

Health Connect and Apple Health

If you have titra+ and switch on Import weight from Health Connect (Android) or Import weight from Apple Health (iOS), titra asks for permission to read your weight, and nothing else. It never writes to Health Connect or Apple Health and never reads any other type of data. Weigh-ins are copied into titra's database on your phone, from your first logged dose onwards, each time you open the app. They are treated like weigh-ins you typed: they leave your phone only if you also use cloud backup. Data from Health Connect and Apple Health is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone else. Switch the import off in titra, or remove titra's access in Health Connect or the iOS Health app, at any time; weigh-ins already copied stay in titra until you delete them.

We do not use advertising networks, attribution SDKs or the advertising identifier. On Android the advertising-ID permission is removed from the app.

Legal bases (UK GDPR / EU GDPR)

  • Health data in cloud backup: your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)), given when you turn on cloud backup. You can withdraw it by signing out and deleting your account.
  • Usage analytics: your consent, given with the in-app switch, which is off by default.
  • Crash reporting: our legitimate interest in a working app. Crash reports contain no health entries.
  • Subscriptions and your account: performance of our contract with you.

International transfers

Cloud backup data is stored in Google's Europe multi-region. Google, Crashlytics, Analytics and RevenueCat may process data in the United States. Where data leaves the UK or EEA we rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and otherwise on Standard Contractual Clauses with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

How long we keep it

  • Cloud backup and account: until you delete your account.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Analytics events: up to 14 months (Google Analytics retention setting).
  • Purchase records: as long as needed for billing, refunds and legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

  • Use titra without an account; nothing is uploaded.
  • Export everything as CSV, or delete everything, from You in the app.
  • Delete your cloud account in the app (You → Cloud backup → Delete account) or by email — see how to delete your account.
  • You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email info@titrapp.com. You can complain to the Polish data protection authority (UODO), the UK ICO, or your local authority.
  • US residents, including under Washington's My Health My Data Act: we do not sell or share consumer health data, and you can request access or deletion at the same address.

Security

Data sent to Google and RevenueCat is encrypted in transit, and Google encrypts stored data at rest. Cloud backup is not end-to-end encrypted: Google holds the keys. On your phone the data is protected by your device's own security.

Children

titra is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above. Material changes are announced in the app.
