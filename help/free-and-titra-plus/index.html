What is free in titra and what titra+ adds

Everything you need to keep the log is free. titra+ is an optional subscription that adds views built from your log, weight import, a PDF summary and cloud backup.

Everything you need to keep the log is free in titra: logging doses, reminders, the injection-site map, the timeline, side effects, food noise and CSV export. titra+ is an optional subscription that adds views built from your log, weight import from your phone's health app, a PDF summary and cloud backup.

What is free

  • The Today screen, with the dose ring and the count of days to your next dose
  • One-tap dose logging, including logging a dose with an earlier date and time, and editing or deleting any entry
  • Reminders, with the name of your medication hidden on the lock screen by default
  • The injection-site map and its zoomed grid
  • The journey chart: weight, dose steps and side effects on one timeline
  • Weight in kilograms or pounds, and a goal weight if you want to set one
  • Side effects with how strong and how long, shown beside how often each was reported in the prescribing information and on placebo
  • A daily food-noise rating from 1 to 5
  • Where your current dose sits among the dose steps on your medicine's label
  • An optional line showing the published trial average for your medication, with its source
  • CSV export of your full history, and Delete all data
  • All 24 languages, with no account and no ads

None of that is limited by time or by a number of entries.

What titra+ adds

titra+ feature What it shows or does
Trials Your logged weight change beside the published average at the end of the trials for your medication, with the placebo figure and the source. For comparison only.
Side-effect patterns Which days after a dose your side effects land, and how this dose step compares with the one before.
Dose-step review Each dose step you have logged: how long you were on it, your weight change and your side-effect days.
Food noise rhythm How your food-noise ratings move across the days after a dose.
Medication level illustration A curve of how your medication rises after each dose and clears, drawn from the half-life on its label. It is an illustration, not a measurement of your levels.
Weight import Reads weight from Apple Health on iPhone or Health Connect on Android. Weight only, read only.
PDF summary A report of your dose steps, weights, doses and side effects to share with your prescriber.
Cloud backup and sync Backs your log up to your account so it can be restored on another phone. Sign-in required.

The first five live in the Insights tab. Without titra+ you can still open that tab and read what each view would show.

Insights opens with the line "Patterns from your own log. Nothing here is advice: take questions to your prescriber." That holds for all of titra+. It rearranges what you logged and sets published figures beside it. It does not interpret either one.

How titra+ is billed

titra+ is a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play. The titra+ screen in the app shows the plans and the prices exactly as the store supplies them for your country, before you confirm anything. Payment goes through the store, and we never see your card details [3].

A subscription renews until you cancel it, and you manage or cancel it in your store account, not in titra [1][2]. Deleting the app does not cancel it, and neither does deleting your titra account.

The titra+ screen has a Restore purchases button for when you reinstall or change phone. It works without an account. If you do sign in, titra+ is tied to that account as well, so it can follow you to a new phone.

What happens if you stop

Your log stays exactly as it is. Logging and everything in the free list carries on working, and nothing is removed from your phone.

The titra+ parts close again: the Insights views, weight import, the PDF summary and backing up. Weigh-ins that were already imported stay in your log. A cloud copy made while you subscribed stays until you delete your account [3].

Why some of it is paid

titra has no ads and we do not sell data, so the app has to be paid for some other way. titra+ is that way. We drew the line so that the record itself is always free and always yours to export: what costs money is the extra work done on top of it.

For what each side does with your data, see your data, export and delete.

Quick answers

Is logging really free?

Yes. Logging doses, weights, side effects and food noise is free, with no limit on entries, and so are reminders, the injection-site map, the timeline and CSV export.

How much does titra+ cost?

The App Store or Google Play shows the current price for your country on the titra+ screen before you pay. We do not print prices here, because the stores set them by country and they change.

Do I need an account for titra+?

Not to subscribe or to restore a purchase. You need to sign in only for cloud backup, because the backup has to know whose log it is.

Does titra+ add advice?

No. The titra+ views are built from your own log, and the Insights tab says so at the top. Questions go to your prescriber.
