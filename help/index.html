About these articles

Every article here answers one question about how titra works, and answers it from the app's code, not from memory. We name the version we checked against and the date we checked.

They stop where the app stops. titra records what you log and never recommends, so these pages explain what a screen shows and why, and leave decisions about your medication to you and your prescriber.

Found a mistake? Write to info@titrapp.com. Corrections are dated and listed in the changelog.

Quick answers

Do these articles give medical advice?

No. They describe how the app behaves and nothing more. They never say what to do with a dose or a symptom: that is for your prescriber and the leaflet that comes with your medicine.

How do you know the articles are right?

Each one is written from the app's own code and checked against the current version before it goes up. The date of the last check is on the page, and every change is logged.

What if my app behaves differently?

Check the version at the bottom of the You tab first, since an older version may differ. If it still does not match, write to info@titrapp.com and we will fix the page or the app.
