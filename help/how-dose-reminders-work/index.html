Using titra

How titra dose reminders work

titra reminds you at one time of day, on the day your next dose is due. It counts from the dose you last logged, and it keeps going if a dose is missed.

How the due date is worked out

Every medication in titra carries the interval from its label: seven days for a weekly medicine, one day for a daily one. The next due date is the date of your last logged dose plus that interval. The reminder fires at your reminder time on that date.

So the schedule is anchored to your log, not to a fixed weekday. If your last logged dose was on a Tuesday and your medicine is weekly, the next reminder is the following Tuesday. Edit that entry to say Monday, and the reminder moves to Monday. The hour you logged at makes no difference, because the time always comes from your setting.

The reminder time starts at 09:00. You can change it during setup or later in the You tab, under Reminders.

Until a first dose is logged, titra has nothing to count from and plans no reminders. If you tell the setup screens you are already on your medication, they ask when you last took a dose and log it for you, which starts the count.

What happens after a missed dose

Nothing stops. If the due time passes and no dose has been logged, titra sets the next reminder for today at your reminder time. If that time has already gone, it sets it for an hour from now. From there the plan carries on at your usual interval.

The plan is rebuilt whenever you open the app, so while a dose stays unlogged, opening titra sets a fresh reminder. Meanwhile the ring on the Today screen shows how many days late the dose is. Log it, and everything is timed again from the new entry.

titra has no view on a late dose. It counts the days and leaves the question of what to do to you, your prescriber and the leaflet.

How far ahead reminders are planned

Reminders are notifications that titra schedules on your phone. They are not messages sent from a server, so they need no account and no connection.

titra plans them 28 days ahead and rebuilds the whole plan each time you open the app, log a dose or change a reminder setting. There is a consequence worth knowing: if the app is not opened at all for four weeks, the planned reminders run out, and they start again the next time you open it.

What a reminder says

With the default settings a reminder reads:

Medication reminder

Your scheduled dose is due. Tap to log it.

The name of your medication is left out on purpose, so it is not on show on a lock screen. The setting is called Hide medication name on lock screen, it lives in the You tab under Reminders, and it starts switched on. Switch it off and the reminder is titled with the name of your medication, and the line under it names the medication too.

At the moment the reminder text is in English whichever language the app is set to.

Tapping a reminder opens the dose log, with your current dose and a suggested site already filled in.

If reminders do not arrive

Reminders depend on your phone letting titra send notifications. The app asks for that permission during setup, and again if you switch Dose reminders on later.

If notifications are turned off for titra, the Today screen and the You tab both show a line saying "Notifications are off for titra, so reminders can't reach you", with a shortcut to the right place in your phone's settings. On Android the reminders have their own notification category, named Dose reminders.

Beyond that, the phone is in charge. Its own notification settings decide whether a reminder makes a sound, shows on the lock screen or is held back, and Apple and Google explain those settings in their help pages [1][2].

What reminders do not do

  • There is one reminder time. You cannot set a different time for different days.
  • A reminder has no buttons. You cannot put it off from the notification, only open the app from it.
  • titra knows whether a dose was logged. It cannot know whether a dose was taken.
  • Switching Dose reminders off in the You tab cancels every planned reminder until you switch it back on.

Quick answers

Do titra reminders need an account or a connection?

No. They are ordinary notifications that titra schedules on the phone itself, so they work with no account and with no connection.

Why did my reminder day move?

Because reminders count from the date of the dose you last logged. Log a dose a day later than usual and the next reminder is a day later too.

Can a reminder show the name of my medication?

Yes, if you want it to. Turn off Hide medication name on lock screen in the You tab under Reminders. It is on by default, so the name is hidden until you change it.

Will titra remind me again after a missed dose?

Yes. A missed dose does not stop the plan. The next reminder is set for today at your reminder time, or an hour from now if that time has passed.
