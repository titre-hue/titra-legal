titra is a GLP-1 dose tracker for iPhone and Android, made by AgentM: it records the doses, weights and side effects you log, and never gives dosing or medical advice. This page says what that means in practice, what the app deliberately leaves out, and who is behind it.

What titra is

A logbook that answers three questions on one screen: when is my next dose, where did I inject last, and how am I doing.

You log a dose with one tap, because the sheet opens with your current dose and a suggested site already filled in. From that entry titra works out when the next dose is due, fills a ring on the Today screen as the days pass, and reminds you at the time you chose. For injections, a body map keeps track of where each one went.

Add weigh-ins and side effects when they happen and the journey chart puts all three on the same dates: weight as a line, each dose step as a shaded band behind it, side effects along the bottom. That is the whole idea. The log is simple to keep, and the picture builds from it.

The app is free to download and works without an account. An optional subscription, titra+, adds views built from your log, weight import, a PDF summary and cloud backup. What is free and what titra+ adds lists both sides.

What titra is not

  • titra never gives dosing or medical advice.
  • titra never shows ads or sells your data.
  • titra never needs an account to keep your log.

titra is a personal logging tool. It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and it does not give dosing advice.

In the app that rule looks like this:

  • It never says how much to take or when to change dose. The dose steps it shows are the ones printed on your medicine's label, shown for reference.
  • It passes no verdict on your numbers. The PDF summary states what you logged and nothing else.
  • The medication level view in titra+ is an illustration drawn from the half-life on the label. It is not a measurement of your levels.
  • Figures from prescribing information and published trials are shown with their source, for comparison only.

titra is also not connected to any medicine maker. It does not sell, prescribe or supply medication, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by the companies whose medicines it can log.

There are no ads in it. We use no advertising networks and no attribution SDK, and we do not sell personal or health data [1].

Who makes it

We are the team at AgentM, an independent software company, and titra is our app. We decide what goes into it and we write and check the pages on this site.

titra+ subscriptions pay for the work. That is why the app can do without ads, and why the free part is the part you need every day: logging, reminders, the site map, the timeline and export.

Pages here are signed "the titra team" because the work is the team's. Our author page explains how each page is checked. If anything on this site or in the app is wrong, tell us at info@titrapp.com.

Fact sheet

What it is
A GLP-1 dose tracker: dose log, reminders, injection-site map and a timeline of weight, dose steps and side effects.
For
Weekly injections, daily injections and daily tablets.
Platforms
iPhone and iPad (App Store) and Android 8.0 or later (Google Play). App ID com.titra.app on both.
Languages
24 app languages. This site is in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian and Polish.
Account
Not needed. An account is used only for cloud backup.
Ads
None. No advertising networks, no attribution SDK, no advertising identifier.
Cost
Free to download. titra+ is an optional subscription, billed by the App Store or Google Play.
Where your log is stored
In a database on your phone. It is uploaded to Google Firestore only if you turn on titra+ cloud backup.
Crash reports
Always sent, through Firebase Crashlytics. They contain no health entries.
Usage analytics
An in-app switch that is off by default.
Export and deletion
CSV export is free. Delete all data clears the phone; deleting your account removes the cloud copy.
Maker
AgentM sp. z o.o., registered in Poland.
Contact
info@titrapp.com
Legal
Privacy policy, terms of service, delete your account
