Delete Your titra Account and Data

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This page explains how to delete your titra account and everything stored with it. It applies to the titra app published by AgentM on Google Play and the Apple App Store (com.titra.app).

If you never created an account, there is no account or cloud backup to delete. titra works fully without an account. In that case your log is stored only on your phone, and uninstalling the app removes it.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open titra and go to the You tab.
  2. Tap Cloud backup, make sure you are signed in, then tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm when prompted.

If you are signed out, sign in first so the app knows which cloud account to delete, or use Option 2 below.

Deletion runs immediately. Your cloud data is removed first, then your sign-in account. If the first step does not complete, your sign-in account is kept so that you can try again.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can no longer sign in — for example you lost access to the email address or Apple ID you signed up with — email info@titrapp.com from the address associated with your account, with the subject Account deletion request.

We will verify the request, action it, and confirm by reply. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days, as required under UK GDPR and EU GDPR.

What is not deleted, and why

  • Data on your own phone. Deleting your account removes what is stored in the cloud; your log remains on your phone so titra keeps working without an account. To remove it too, use You → Delete all data (which also removes the titra 1.x copy of your log, if you updated from 1.x) or uninstall the app.
  • Your subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, not by us, and deleting your account does not cancel one. Cancel through the App Store or Google Play before deleting, otherwise billing continues. See Apple or Google.
  • Promo code redemption records. If you redeemed a promo code in titra 1.x, the code stays marked as used so it cannot be reused. The record keeps your account identifier; email us and we will remove it.
  • Purchase history held by RevenueCat. Our subscription provider keeps your purchase records under your account identifier and, on iPhone and iPad, a device identifier, as needed for billing and refunds. Email us to request their deletion.
  • Crash reports and analytics. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are kept for 90 days and may carry your account identifier; email us to have them removed sooner. Usage analytics are collected only if you switched them on (in versions before 2.0.2, Google's library may also have recorded the first time the app was opened). They are not linked to your account and expire under Google Analytics retention. You can switch analytics off at any time in the app.
  • Records we must keep by law. Where a legal or accounting obligation requires retention, we keep only the minimum for the required period.

How long deletion takes

In-app deletion is immediate and irreversible. Email requests are actioned once verified and within 30 days at the latest. Routine backups may retain copies for a short period before rolling off; those copies are not used for any other purpose.
