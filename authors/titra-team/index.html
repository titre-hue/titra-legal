The titra team: who writes these pages

The titra teamThe team at AgentM that makes titra

titra is a GLP-1 dose tracker for iPhone and Android, made by AgentM: it records the doses, weights and side effects you log, and never gives dosing or medical advice. We are the team at AgentM that builds it, and we write every page on this site. Pages are signed "the titra team" because the work is the team's, not one person's.

Our one rule

titra records what you log and never recommends. The site keeps to the same rule.

A page here may explain what a screen shows, how a date is worked out, where your data is stored or what a button removes. It will not tell you what to do with a dose or a symptom, and it will not say what result to expect from a medicine. Those are questions for your prescriber and for the leaflet that comes with your medication.

How a page is checked

We write from the app, not from memory.

Before a page goes up, each statement about titra is traced to the place in the app's code or its privacy policy that makes it true [1], and the page is read against the current version. Help articles name that version and carry the date of the check.

Where a statement rests on someone else's document, such as a medicine's label or a store's help page, the page links to it and says when we read it. A test of ours: if we cannot point to the line that proves a sentence, the sentence comes out.

Every page shows when it was published and when it last changed, and each real change gets a line in the changelog.

What we will not publish

  • Advice about doses, timing, side effects or switching medicines
  • Promises about weight or any other outcome
  • Reviews, ratings, user numbers or quotes from users
  • Prices: the App Store and Google Play show those before you pay
  • Medicine brand names in page titles, headings or images
  • Claims about security or privacy that the app's code does not bear out

Corrections

If a page is wrong, or the app does something a page says it does not, write to info@titrapp.com. We fix the page or the app, and we log the change with its date.

Who stands behind this

Agent M sp. z o.o., an independent company registered in Poland, makes titra and is the data controller named in its privacy policy [1]. titra is not affiliated with or endorsed by any medicine maker. There is more on the about page, and the articles we have written are on the help page.
